## 2026-09-28 · redesign session 4: Article template, Explore page, series (on branch redesign, not live yet)
- **What changed:** Three things. (1) A real way to group articles into a series was added — there's now one real series, "Beginner Basics" (the ratio article, then the pour-over article, in that order), and it now shows up on the homepage's "Series" section, which was built hidden last session. A second series slot, "Troubleshooting Deep Dives," exists as a placeholder ready for when there's enough content for it. (2) Every article page got a real redesign: a colored topic badge at the top, a thin progress bar that fills in as you scroll, a clickable list of the article's own headings that follows you down the page, the author's name and a short bio at the end (currently placeholder text — replace it at /keystatic under "Author" whenever you're ready), and three "Keep reading" suggestions styled as proper cards instead of a plain list. Nothing about the newsletter sign-up boxes changed. (3) The "Articles" page was rebuilt from a plain list into a real browse page: six topic tiles (in the same colors as the homepage's "Start here" tiles), a set of tag links, and the series list — no more flat "every article" feed there, since the homepage and topic/tag pages already cover that. New pages were also added for browsing by tag. Along the way, a genuine contrast problem was found and fixed: on one of the topic tiles ("Sourcing"), the label text was too faint to meet accessibility contrast minimums — that's fixed everywhere now, not just on that one tile.
- **Why:** Session 4 of the six-session redesign rollout in docs/UPDATE-WORKFLOW.md section 7.
- **How to undo:** this is on the redesign branch, not merged — reset the branch to the prior commit (c748ec7) to remove this whole session. Nothing on the live site is affected either way.
